How it works

Three steps to your first piece.

  1. Connect your rower

    Any rower with Bluetooth FTMS, or a Concept2 PM5. Add a heart-rate strap or a watch that broadcasts heart rate.

  2. Pick a workout

    Today's suggestion, one of 21 built-in workouts, a program day or your own.

  3. Row

    The arc pacer swings through drive and recovery at your target stroke rate. The screen tells you whether you're too hard, in zone or too easy.

The workout screen

Made to be read from the seat.

  • Pace zones from your 2k. UT2 to AN, with live feedback on every stroke.

  • Race a ghost of your best or last session, with the gap in metres.

  • Voice, beeps and metronome, so you don't have to watch the screen on long pieces.

  • Start on the first stroke, auto pause when you stop, quick settings without leaving.

  • Portrait or landscape, keeps going with the screen off or in picture-in-picture.

FAQ

Questions.

Does it work with my rower?

If your rower or its console speaks Bluetooth FTMS (most recent Bluetooth rowers do), yes. The Concept2 PM5 is supported directly. Not sure? Install it and tap the Rower button: ErgBeat lists what it finds, and there's a demo rower to try everything without a machine.

Do I need an account?

No. There's no account and no sign-in.

Where is my data?

On your phone. You can back it up to a file you choose (for example on Google Drive) and export sessions as FIT or CSV.

Can I use it with Strava or Garmin Connect?

Yes. The Garmin watch app records straight to Garmin Connect, and Pro exports FIT files for Strava, Garmin Connect and intervals.icu.

Does it work with the screen off?

Yes. The workout keeps running with the screen off, with a notification on the lock screen.

Which languages?

English, Croatian, German, Spanish, French, Italian, Portuguese, Polish and Dutch.

ErgBeat for Garmin

The watch shows your workout and records it to Garmin Connect, with the numbers from your rower, heart rate from your wrist and a lap for every interval. It works together with the ErgBeat app on your Android phone.

ErgBeat isn't in the Connect IQ store yet (it will be once the phone app is on Google Play), so for now you install it by hand. It takes two minutes.

1. Download the file for your watch

  • Forerunner 265 Download
  • Forerunner 965 Download
  • fēnix 8, 47 mm (AMOLED) Download
  • fēnix 8, 43 mm Download
  • fēnix 8 Pro, 47 mm Download
  • fēnix 8 Solar, 47 mm Download
  • fēnix 8 Solar, 51 mm Download

Your watch isn't listed? Write to jaan.dsgn@gmail.com and tell me which one you have.

2. Copy it to the watch

  1. Connect the watch to a computer with its USB cable.
  2. Open the watch like a USB drive. On Windows it shows up in File Explorer. On a Mac you need a free tool such as OpenMTP, because Garmin watches don't show up in Finder.
  3. Copy the file into the GARMIN/APPS folder and unplug the watch.

3. Connect it to the phone

  1. You need Garmin Connect and the ErgBeat app on the same Android phone, with the watch paired in Garmin Connect.
  2. In ErgBeat: Settings → Garmin watch app → turn on ErgBeat on your Garmin.
  3. Open ErgBeat on the watch before you start rowing, then start the workout on the phone.

If something goes wrong

If the watch shows an IQ! icon, the watch app crashed. Connect the watch to the computer and send me the file GARMIN/APPS/LOGS/CIQ_LOG.YML: it shows exactly where it happened.

An app installed this way doesn't update itself. When a new version is out, download the file again and replace the old one.

ErgBeat za Garmin

Sat prikazuje trening i snima ga u Garmin Connect, s brojkama s veslača, pulsom sa zapešća i krugom za svaki interval. Radi zajedno s aplikacijom ErgBeat na Android mobitelu.

ErgBeat još nije u Connect IQ trgovini (bit će kad aplikacija za mobitel izađe na Google Play), pa se zasad instalira ručno. Traje dvije minute.

1. Preuzmi datoteku za svoj sat

Popis satova je gore, u engleskom dijelu: dodirni svoj sat i datoteka se preuzme.

2. Kopiraj je na sat

  1. Spoji sat na računalo USB kabelom.
  2. Otvori sat kao USB disk. Na Windowsima se vidi u File Exploreru. Na Macu treba besplatan alat poput OpenMTP, jer se Garmin satovi ne vide u Finderu.
  3. Kopiraj datoteku u mapu GARMIN/APPS i odspoji sat.

3. Poveži ga s mobitelom

  1. Na istom Android mobitelu trebaju biti Garmin Connect i ErgBeat, a sat uparen u Garmin Connectu.
  2. U ErgBeatu: Postavke → Aplikacija za Garmin sat → uključi ErgBeat na Garmin satu.
  3. Otvori ErgBeat na satu prije nego kreneš veslati, pa pokreni trening na mobitelu.

Ako nešto ne radi

Ako sat pokaže ikonu IQ!, aplikacija na satu se srušila. Spoji sat na računalo i pošalji mi datoteku GARMIN/APPS/LOGS/CIQ_LOG.YML na jaan.dsgn@gmail.com: iz nje se vidi gdje je točno puklo.

Ovako instalirana aplikacija ne ažurira se sama. Kad izađe nova verzija, preuzmi datoteku ponovno i zamijeni staru.

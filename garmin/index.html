ErgBeat for Garmin

The watch shows your workout and records it to Garmin Connect, with the numbers from your rower, heart rate from your wrist and a lap for every interval. It works together with the ErgBeat app on your Android phone.

1. Install it from the Connect IQ store

Open ErgBeat in the Connect IQ store

Or search for ErgBeat in the Connect IQ app on your phone. It works with Forerunner 965 and 265 and all fēnix 8 models, and it updates itself.

Installed ErgBeat by hand before? The store version is a separate app, so remove the old one first: in Garmin Connect, your watch → Activities & Apps → ErgBeat → Remove.

2. Connect it to the phone

  1. You need Garmin Connect and the ErgBeat app on the same Android phone, with the watch paired in Garmin Connect.
  2. In ErgBeat: Settings → Garmin watch app → turn on ErgBeat on your Garmin.
  3. Open ErgBeat on the watch before you start rowing, then start the workout on the phone.

If something goes wrong

If the watch shows an IQ! icon, the watch app crashed. Open ErgBeat on the watch once more with the phone nearby, then tap Send feedback in the phone app. If you can, also connect the watch to a computer and send me the file GARMIN/APPS/LOGS/CIQ_LOG.YML at jaan.dsgn@gmail.com: it shows exactly where it happened.

Test versions (by hand)

For testers only: these files sometimes carry a fix before it reaches the store. They don't update themselves; copy the file into GARMIN/APPS over the USB cable (on a Mac with OpenMTP). Don't keep this and the store version on the watch at the same time.

ErgBeat za Garmin

Sat prikazuje trening i snima ga u Garmin Connect, s brojkama s veslača, pulsom sa zapešća i krugom za svaki interval. Radi zajedno s aplikacijom ErgBeat na Android mobitelu.

1. Instaliraj je iz Connect IQ trgovine

Otvori ErgBeat u Connect IQ trgovini

Ili u aplikaciji Connect IQ na mobitelu potraži ErgBeat. Radi s Forerunnerom 965 i 265 i svim fēnixima 8, a ažurira se sama.

Već si ErgBeat instalirao ručno? Verzija iz trgovine je zasebna aplikacija, pa prvo makni staru: u Garmin Connectu tvoj sat → Aktivnosti i aplikacije → ErgBeat → Ukloni.

2. Poveži je s mobitelom

  1. Na istom Android mobitelu trebaju biti Garmin Connect i ErgBeat, a sat uparen u Garmin Connectu.
  2. U ErgBeatu: Postavke → Aplikacija za Garmin sat → uključi ErgBeat na Garmin satu.
  3. Otvori ErgBeat na satu prije nego kreneš veslati, pa pokreni trening na mobitelu.

Ako nešto ne radi

Ako sat pokaže ikonu IQ!, aplikacija na satu se srušila. Otvori ErgBeat na satu još jednom uz mobitel, pa u aplikaciji na mobitelu dodirni Pošalji povratnu informaciju. Ako možeš, spoji sat i na računalo i pošalji mi datoteku GARMIN/APPS/LOGS/CIQ_LOG.YML na jaan.dsgn@gmail.com: iz nje se vidi gdje je točno puklo.

Testne verzije (ručno)

Samo za testere: ove datoteke ponekad imaju popravak prije trgovine. Ne ažuriraju se same; kopiraj datoteku u GARMIN/APPS preko USB kabela (na Macu uz OpenMTP). Popis datoteka je gore, u engleskom dijelu. Nemoj na satu držati i ovu i verziju iz trgovine.

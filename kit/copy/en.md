## How it works

1. **Connect your rower.** Any rower with Bluetooth FTMS, or a Concept2 PM5. Add a heart-rate strap
   or a watch that broadcasts heart rate.
2. **Pick a workout.** Today's suggestion, one of 21 built-in workouts, a program day or your own.
3. **Row.** The arc pacer swings through drive and recovery at your target stroke rate; the screen
   tells you whether you're too hard, in zone or too easy.

## The workout screen

### Made to be read from the seat.
- **Arc pacer:** a bead swings through each stroke at your target rate.
- **Pace zones from your 2k:** UT2 to AN, with "too hard / in zone / too easy" live.
- **Race a ghost** of your best or last session, with the gap in metres.
- **Voice, beeps and metronome**, so you don't have to watch the screen on long pieces.
- **Start on the first stroke**, auto pause when you stop, quick settings without leaving.
- **Portrait or landscape**, keeps going with the screen off or in picture-in-picture.

## Workouts and programs

### Row with a plan.
- **21 workouts:** steady state, intervals, sprints, pyramid, rate ladder, CrossFit-style WODs with
  burpees or wall balls, and 500 m, 1k, 2k, 5k and 30-minute tests.
- **Four programs**, four weeks each, paced from your own 2k: *Foundations*, *Build*, *Peak* and
  *CrossFit Engine*. Every one ends with a 2k test.
- **Workout editor:** build your own, with intervals, rests and exercises off the rower. Share it
  as a link or QR code.

## Garmin

### On your wrist too.
**ErgBeat for Garmin** is a free Connect IQ app. The watch shows the workout and records it to

interval.

Works with fēnix 8 (43 mm, 47 mm, Pro 47 mm, Solar 47 mm and 51 mm), Forerunner 965 and

## FAQ

**Does it work with my rower?**
If your rower or its console speaks Bluetooth FTMS (most recent Bluetooth rowers do), yes. The
Concept2 PM5 is supported directly. Not sure? Install it and tap the Rower button: ErgBeat lists
what it finds, and there's a demo rower to try everything without a machine.

**Do I need an account?**
No. There's no account and no sign-in.

**Where is my data?**
On your phone. You can back it up to a file you choose (for example on Google Drive) and export
sessions as FIT or CSV.

**Can I use it with Strava or Garmin Connect?**
Yes: the Garmin watch app records straight to Garmin Connect, and Pro exports FIT files for
Strava, Garmin Connect and intervals.icu.

**Does it work with the screen off?**
Yes. The workout keeps running with the screen off, with a notification on the lock screen.

**Which languages?**
English, Croatian, German, Spanish, French, Italian, Portuguese, Polish and Dutch.
